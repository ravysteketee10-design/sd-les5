Hamburger Pattie <br> 
▢800g – 1kg / 1.6 – 2 lb ground beef (mince), 20%+ fat (Note 1) <br> 
▢Salt and pepper <br> 
▢3 onions, peeled and sliced into rings <br> 
▢2 tbsp oil <br> 
▢4 – 8 slices cheese of choice, I use Swiss (optional) <br> 
Hamburger <br> 
▢4 soft hamburger buns, lightly toasted <br> 
▢Lettuce, tomato slices <br> 
▢Ketchup, mustard, relish, sliced pickles – or try my Special Burger Sauce! <br> 
On the side <br> 
▢French fries <br> 
▢Baked potato wedges <br> 

Cook Mode <br> 
Prevent screen from sleeping <br> 
Instructions <br> 
Separate beef into 4 equal portions. Use hands to lightly form into patties the size of your buns (mine are about 10 cm / 4″) – don’t press hard, light fingers = soft juicy patties (see video for how I do this). <br> 
Season generously with salt and pepper on both sides. Make a dent on one side (stop burger from become dome shape and shrinking when cooking). <br> 
Heat 1 tbsp oil in a heavy based skillet or BBQ over high heat. Add onion and cook until wilted and caramelised. Season with salt and pepper, then remove. <br> 
Heat 1 tbsp oil until smoking. Add patties and cook for 2 minutes until deep golden with a great crust. Do not press! Flip carefully, cook for 1 minute then top with cheese (if using). Cover with lid and cook for further 1 minute until cheese is melted.
Meanwhile, toast the cut side of the buns lightly. <br> 
To serve: Spread base of buns with sauce / condiment of choice. Top with lettuce then tomato, then hamburger patty. Pile over onions, sliced pickles, then more sauce/condiments. Top with lid of bun. Serve immediately. <br>
